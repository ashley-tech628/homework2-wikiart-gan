# Conditional GAN trained from scratch

This directory contains the independently implemented model for the full WikiArt experiment. It is distinct from the pretrained StyleGAN2 study: the generator and discriminator here begin with random weights, and the style label is used as a condition. The final DataHub run trained at 128 × 128 resolution on 73,300 paintings from the 81,444-image assigned collection, holding out 8,144 images. It completed 40 epochs and 45,800 updates.

`model.py` defines a convolutional generator and a spectrally normalized projection discriminator. `train.py` implements hinge-loss optimization, Adam updates, generator exponential moving average (EMA), checkpointing, and fixed-noise sample grids. `prepare.py` indexes and prepares the complete assigned dataset; `evaluate.py` loads the EMA generator and records Inception Score and both pool3 and Ignite-default FID; `sample.py` generates images from a selected style label. The recorded hyperparameters are in [`../results/full_datahub_config.json`](../results/full_datahub_config.json).

On 1,000 generated images and 1,000 held-out real images, the completed run records **pool3 FID 177.32** and **IS 2.405**. The [generated grid](../results/full_datahub_generated_grid.png) has painterly color and texture but limited subject coherence. The [loss CSV](../results/full_datahub_losses.csv), [loss plot](../results/full_datahub_loss_curves.png), [metric JSON](../results/full_datahub_metrics.json), and [file-level source manifest](../results/full_datahub_full_provenance.json) are preserved for inspection.

The model expects a directory of style subfolders under `--data`. The training and evaluation entry points expose their options through `--help`; dependencies are listed in [`requirements.txt`](requirements.txt), and the report records the settings of the completed run. Prepared images and checkpoints were stored in DataHub temporary storage, and the large scratch-model checkpoint is not part of this Git repository.
